The gods built riddles. Mortals built machines.

Five trials await
  1. I

    The Antikythera Mechanism

    Rebuild the world's first computer. Mesh bronze gears to chart sun, moon and stars, and unlock what the heavens hide.

  2. II

    The Labyrinth

    Shifting walls, one thread, and something breathing in the dark. Outwit the Minotaur before it finds you.

  3. III

    Archimedes' Burning Mirrors

    Angle polished bronze to bend the sun into one searing beam. Light the beacons. Burn the fleet.

  4. IV

    Leonardo's Aerial Screw

    A flying machine born centuries too early. Wind the spiral sail and lift the puzzle into the third dimension.

  5. V

    The Arena

    Steel, sand and a roaring crowd. Tactical gladiator duels where every strike is a puzzle to solve.
